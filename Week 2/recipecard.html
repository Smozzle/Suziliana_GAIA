Blueberry Cheesecake

Prep Time: 30 minutes | Cook Time: 55 minutes

Ingredients:

  • 200g digestive biscuits
  • 100g melted butter
  • 500g cream cheese
  • 100g sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 150g fresh blueberries
  • 2 tablespoons blueberry jam

Instructions:

1. Crush the digestive biscuits and mix them with melted butter. Press the mixture into the bottom of a cake pan to form the crust.
2. Beat the cream cheese, sugar, eggs and vanilla extract until smooth. Pour the mixture over the biscuit crust.
3. Bake at 170°C for approximately 45 minutes. Allow the cheesecake to cool before adding the blueberry topping.
4. Mix the blueberries with blueberry jam and spread them over the cheesecake. Chill before serving.

Difficulty: Medium
